# Code of Conduct

General rules everyone here has to follow. Depending on how bad it is, you get a warning - or an
instant ban with no warning at all.

These rules apply everywhere the project goes: this repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and any chat, forum or other space the project runs in future. A ban covers all of it,
not just the place it happened.

**Nazis, fascists and comparable idiots have no place here.** That includes denying the Holocaust or
any other genocide, and glorifying war. Not up for discussion.

Other than that, the following are off limits - which really ought to go without saying:

- Racism of any kind - antisemitism and anti-Palestinian racism both count, and neither one excuses
  the other
- Bullying
- Conspiracy nonsense
- Sexism, anti-LGBTQIA+ garbage
- Insults
- Doxxing - publishing someone's private information without their permission. That applies to third
  parties just as much as to people involved here
- Spam and self-promotion - no advertising your product, project, service or channel here

Short version: **just don't be an asshole.**

## Reporting

Report anything of the above to the maintainers - via a GitHub issue, or privately if you'd rather
not do it in public. Reports are taken seriously, and the maintainers decide on warning vs. ban.
